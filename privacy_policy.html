BabyBloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

📌 Summary: BabyBloom stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, share, or sell any personal information. Your baby's data stays on your phone.

1. Information We Collect

BabyBloom is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect any personal information.

All data you enter in the app (feeding logs, sleep records, health information, baby photos, etc.) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (AsyncStorage).

🔒 We never transmit your data to our servers or any third parties.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following information may be stored locally on your device:

  • Baby name, date of birth, and gender
  • Feeding logs (time, amount, type)
  • Sleep records
  • Health data (temperature, medicine, vaccines, drops)
  • Baby photos (stored locally)
  • App preferences (language, dark mode, premium status)
  • Alarm and reminder settings

This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

3. Notifications & Alarms

BabyBloom uses local notifications to send feeding and pump reminders. These notifications are:

  • Scheduled and delivered entirely on your device
  • Not connected to any external server
  • Only sent if you have granted notification permissions

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

4. Camera & Photo Library

BabyBloom may request access to your camera and photo library for the Photo Journal feature (Premium). Photos are:

  • Stored only on your device
  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Only accessed when you explicitly choose to add a photo

5. Premium Subscription

BabyBloom offers premium subscriptions (Monthly, 6-Month, and Lifetime). Payment processing is handled by Google Play Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

6. Third-Party Services

BabyBloom does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect your data.

✅ No ads · ✅ No tracking · ✅ No data sharing

7. Children's Privacy

BabyBloom is designed for parents and caregivers. While the app tracks information about babies and children, this data is:

  • Entered by parents/caregivers, not by children
  • Stored only on the parent's device
  • Never shared with us or any third party

We comply with applicable children's privacy laws including COPPA.

8. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security settings. We recommend:

  • Using a screen lock on your device
  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date
  • Using the app's backup feature to create copies of your data

9. Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app (all local data will be deleted)
  • Going to device Settings → Apps → BabyBloom → Clear Data

Since we don't store any data on our servers, there is no additional step required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
